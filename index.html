Research

FP-DeErr: application-oriented error decomposition for foundation potentials

University of Maryland, 2024 to present

Paper (arXiv) Code Leaderboard

A framework that evaluates FPs against DFT on the quantities and configurations that decide whether a computational task succeeds, rather than on dataset-averaged error alone. It is applied to three tasks:

  • Force prediction. Metrics for highly accurate, large-error, and far-from-equilibrium atoms, which average MAE/RMSE hides. Leaderboard
  • Phase stability and elemental ordering. Convex-hull agreement and energy-ranking metrics for competing phases and orderings, separating energy-ranking errors from relaxation errors. Leaderboard
  • Ion migration by NEB. FP-NEB benchmarked against DFT-NEB on 154 Li- and Na-ion pathways, tracing barrier errors to the potential energy surface, endpoint relaxation, and along-path forces. Leaderboard

Tools: Python, ASE, MatCalc, pymatgen, SLURM on HPC
