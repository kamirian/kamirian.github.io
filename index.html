Selected research

FP-DeErr: Application-Oriented Error Decomposition for Foundation Potentials

2024 to present

Kiyan Amirian, Ramanuja Srinivasan Saravanan, Felix Adams, Charles E Schwarz, Yifei Mo

First author · arXiv preprint, 2026 · University of Maryland

Preprint Code Leaderboard Overview figure

Designed and developed FP-DeErr, an application-oriented error-decomposition framework for foundation potentials. Co-designed the study and carried out benchmarking against density functional theory (DFT), introducing task-specific metrics to identify where errors arise and how they affect simulation outcomes across three tasks:

  • Force prediction. Designed magnitude- and direction-based diagnostics to expose large errors and far-from-equilibrium failures obscured by average force-error metrics. Leaderboard

  • Phase stability and elemental ordering. Designed convex-hull agreement metrics and evaluated energy rankings to test which phases and atomic orderings a model selects, separating ranking errors from relaxation errors. Leaderboard

  • Ion migration by nudged elastic band (NEB). Designed agreement metrics beyond migration-barrier accuracy to assess FP reliability across 154 Li- and Na-ion pathways, testing energy-profile shape, endpoint energy ranking, and convergence. Decomposed errors into contributions from the energy landscape, endpoint relaxation, and pathway forces. Leaderboard

Models evaluated include UMA, MACE, and CHGNet.

Analytical and numerical instability analysis of functionally graded low-carbon steel

2019 to 2022

Kiyan Amirian, Zahra Abbasi, Ramin Ebrahimi

International Journal of Iron & Steel Society of Iran · 19(2), 66–72 · 2023

Paper PDF DOI

I developed an algorithm to predict tensile instability in functionally graded low-carbon steel, accounting for radial variations in the work-hardening exponent and strength coefficient.

My analysis compared the predicted tensile response with published experimental data and related the calculated strain at maximum load to the cross-section-averaged work-hardening exponent.
